## Why the raw dataset is not here

The final CSV is approximately 149 MB and includes learner-level personal/profile fields. Publishing it to a public repository would be both unnecessary for portfolio review and inappropriate from a data-minimization perspective. The repository therefore demonstrates the work through SQL, documented transformations and aggregate outputs.
