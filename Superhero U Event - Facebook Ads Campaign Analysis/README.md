# Superhero U Event — Facebook Ads Campaign Analysis

A compact marketing analytics case study completed during the Excelerate Data Visualization Early Internship. The project evaluates paid Facebook campaigns for the Superhero U event and turns campaign-performance data into a budget-allocation recommendation.

## Business question

**Which campaigns are underperforming enough to pause or remove, so the remaining marketing budget can be concentrated on stronger campaigns?**

The analysis covered **11 paid campaigns across 13 countries** and compared campaign reach, spend and conversion-efficiency indicators using Excel and Tableau.

## Approach

1. Reviewed and cleaned the campaign-level Excel dataset.
2. Compared campaign reach and cost/performance metrics rather than judging campaigns by spend alone.
3. Identified campaigns that consistently underperformed relative to stronger alternatives.
4. Built a Tableau dashboard to make campaign comparisons easy to review.
5. Recommended discontinuing/reallocating spend from weak campaigns, with an estimated **~30% marketing-budget saving** in the internship scenario.
